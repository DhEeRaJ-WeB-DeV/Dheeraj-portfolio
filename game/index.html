GATE OPENED

The first ward falls behind you. A deeper, moonlit courtyard awaits beyond the gate.

  • Phase 1: get in close and hit its head with Ethe sword button — body shots do nothing.
  • Projectiles flash gold right before they arrive — that's your parry cue. Time Qthe shield button to it.
  • A parried falling blade flies straight back into its head — a free hit, and worth practicing.
  • Below half health it dives into Phase 2: melee stops working, so keep landing parried blades to deal damage.
  • Phase 2 also raises fire-serpents from the floor in a line of lanes — only one lane stays safe. Watch the warning and get there before it erupts.
  • Fire-serpents can also ambush from the sides in Phase 1 — don't let one pin you against a wall.
